# Citrus micro plane: BOM and weight budget

Config: elevon flying wing, pusher motor, ~600 mm span, 2S. Target AUW 125-150 g, well under the 250 g limit.
Control: FS-i6 with its built-in elevon mixer; no flight controller.

All weights and prices are ESTIMATES until measured. Verify before buying.

## Checks before the first flight
1. Confirm the motor's thrust on a 5x3 prop is at least 1.5x the weight (bench test with a scale and watt meter).
2. Balance the CG at the spot the plan gives; adjust with battery position.
3. Elevon mix and direction check with the props off.
4. Range check and failsafe set on the FS-i6.

## Canada
Under 250 g: no registration or pilot certificate needed. Check Transport Canada's current rules before flying.
